# baseball_evaluation_training

Goal: build a baseball statistics framework to measure player performance and team efficiency

Fist objectives:
- Collect and organize player and team statistics
- Develop metrics to evaluate individual player performance, including general and position-specific defensive statistics, batting statistics, and pitching statistics
- Create models to assess team efficiency and strategy
- Provide visualizations and reports for analysis
