# Executive Summary: 12-Week Baseball Training Plan

**Personalised Off-Season Programme**

* **Profile:** 44 · 63 kg · 176 cm · Right-handed Infielder · No Significant Injury History
* **Main Objective:** Build muscle, strength, explosiveness, and throwing velocity while protecting your throwing arm.

## 2. Your Three Gym Sessions

### Monday – Strength and Muscle
* **Squats:** 3 × 8
* **Romanian Deadlift:** 3 × 8
* **Bulgarian Split Squat:** 3 × 8
* **Dumbbell Bench Press:** 3 × 10
* **Chest-Supported Row:** 3 × 10
* **Pallof Press:** 3 × 12

### Wednesday – Upper Body and Arm Health
* **Pull-ups or Lat Pulldown:** 3 × 8–10
* **Incline Dumbbell Press:** 3 × 10
* **Seated Cable Row:** 3 × 10
* **Landmine Press:** 3 × 8
* **Face Pulls:** 2 × 15
* **External Rotations:** 2 × 15
* **Serratus Wall Slides:** 2 × 12

### Friday – Explosiveness
* **Box Jumps:** 3 × 4
* **Trap-Bar Deadlift:** 3 × 5
* **Rotational Medicine-Ball Throws:** 3 × 5 (each side)
* **Step-Ups:** 3 × 8
* **Single-Arm Cable Row:** 3 × 10
* **Lateral Lunges:** 2 × 8
* **Side Plank:** 3 × 30 sec

> **Guidelines:** Keep 2–3 good repetitions in reserve (RIR) on strength exercises. Perform explosive movements quickly, with good technique and full recovery between sets.

## 3. Baseball Field Sessions

### Tuesday – Throwing and Defence
* **Warm-up:** 10–15 minutes
* **Progressive Throwing**
* **10 Controlled Fielding-Position Throws**
* **Lateral Shuffles:** 3 × 10 m
* **First-Step Sprints:** 4 × 5 m
* **Ground-Ball Practice:** 10–15 minutes

### Saturday – Power and Hitting
* **Rotational Medicine-Ball Throws:** 3 × 5 (each side)
* **Short Sprints:** 5 × 10 m
* **Lateral Bounds:** 3 × 4 (each side)
* **Batting Practice:** 15–25 swings
* **Fielding and Reaction Drills:** 10–15 minutes

---

## 4. Twelve-Week Progression

| Weeks | Priority |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1–4** | Build muscle and strength foundation |
| **5–8** | Increase strength and rotational power |
| **9–11** | Explosiveness and baseball performance |
| **12** | Recovery and performance testing |

*Note: Reduce training volume during weeks 4, 8, and 12.*

---

## 5. Throwing-Arm Care

Perform **2–3 times weekly**:
* **Band External Rotation:** 2 × 15
* **Band Internal Rotation:** 2 × 15
* **Face Pulls:** 2 × 15
* **Scapular Push-ups:** 2 × 12
* **Wrist Pronation/Supination:** 2 × 12

> **Key Rules:** Start throwing comfortably and progressively. Keep demanding throwing sessions approximately 48 hours apart. Do not throw through pain or persistent soreness.

Measure at **Weeks 1, 6, and 12**:
* Body weight
* Squat strength
* Pull-up performance
* 10-metre sprint time
